## Laundry Ledger 1.1.0

First public release.

- Note the clothes you give to the laundry in batches, then tick them off as they come back
- Pick Male, Female or Prefer not to say, then choose the clothes you usually send (35 types)
- Remembers your last cloth type and usual quantity for each type
- Home shows batches still at the laundry first, then history with missing pieces flagged
- Settings: edit your clothes, check for updates, one-tap update install
- Fully offline. No account, ads or tracking. Internet is used only to check for updates.
